# Ecosystem Positioning

Operational Resolution is being investigated as a distinct composition layer between heterogeneous representations and downstream operational systems.

Conceptually:

```text
PHYSICAL / DIGITAL SOURCES
          |
          v
      ASSERTIONS
          |
          v
   OPERATIONAL RESOLUTION
          |
     +----+----+
     |    |    |
     v    v    v
   ERP  ATTESTATION  INTELLIGENCE
```

The layer should not replace observation, evidence production, attestation, inference or ERP functionality.

Its proposed responsibility is to resolve their representations for an operational purpose while preserving provenance, conflict and uncertainty.

This positioning is a hypothesis to be tested, not a finalized architecture.
