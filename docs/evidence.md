# Evidence

Evidence is the material associated with an assertion and its provenance.

Examples include:

- fiscal documents;
- sensor readings;
- scanner records;
- photographs or video frames;
- operator records;
- system logs;
- cryptographic proofs;
- external attestations.

The investigation treats evidence as distinct from the assertion it supports.

A resolution should be able to answer:

1. What was asserted?
2. Who or what produced the assertion?
3. What evidence supports it?
4. When and where was it produced?
5. Which rules or context were applied?
6. Why did the system reach the resulting operational state?
