# Experiment 001 — Identity vs Relation

## Question

Can identity and relationships be represented without conflating them?

## Test

```text
Entity: Product X
Relation: Product X located_at Dock 2
Relation: Product X transported_by Vehicle V1
```

## Observation

The entity remains distinct from the relations involving it.

## Result

The distinction appears necessary for the cases tested so far.

This remains a working result, not a formal proof.
