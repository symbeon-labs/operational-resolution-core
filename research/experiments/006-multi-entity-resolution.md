# Experiment 006 — Multi-Entity Resolution

## Question

Can the model handle simultaneous operations involving multiple entities?

## Test

A scenario contains two products, two vehicles, two docks, multiple observations, movement, operator declarations, physical measurement and ERP records.

## Observation

The working model can represent multiple entities, relations, assertions and conflicts without introducing a new primitive for every operational concept.

## Result

The current primitive set remains a viable hypothesis for further testing.
